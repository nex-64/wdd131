My name is Nathan Murray, I am a technology enthusiast from Oklahoma and have a deep love for older electronics. I find them interesting and enjoy working with them. My love for electronics in general started with an old laptop my dad gave me after the backlight stopped working on the screen. I was able to get it working and installed linux on it. The problem solving and learning experiences along the way helped me to realize this is what I love doing and want to continue doing in the future.

Besides technology, I also enjoy baking, fishing, camping, and playing video games. I love to get outside on a nice day and enjoy the weather, and I love to hang out with my friends. Another thing I enjoy doing is traveling. I have driven across the U.S. multiple times and I love the views and unique areas I have been able to visit.
